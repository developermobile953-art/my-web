Web Development in Nairobi

Custom websites and web applications built to be fast, reliable and easy to use — designed for businesses operating in Kenya.

What We Build

We develop websites and web applications that work well on the devices your customers actually use. That means mobile-first layouts, fast loading times even on average connections, and clean code that is easy to maintain later.

Projects range from simple business websites and company profiles to more complex systems such as booking platforms, dashboards, and custom web applications tailored to specific operational needs.

How We Work

  1. 1

    Discovery & Requirements

    We discuss your goals, target audience, features needed, and any existing systems. Clear scope and timeline are agreed before work begins.

  2. 2

    Design & Structure

    Layout, navigation and content structure are planned so the site is easy to use and reflects your business clearly.

  3. 3

    Development

    The site is built with clean, modern code. Responsive behaviour, performance and basic SEO foundations are included by default.

  4. 4

    Testing & Launch

    Thorough checks across devices and browsers. Launch support and a short period of post-launch adjustments are included.

  5. 5

    Ongoing Support

    Optional maintenance, content updates and technical support so the website continues to serve your business well.

Technologies We Commonly Use

Technology choices depend on the project. Common tools include modern HTML, CSS and JavaScript, PHP, MySQL/PostgreSQL, React, Node.js, and established CMS or framework options when they make sense for the client.

The priority is always reliability, maintainability and suitability for the local context — not using a tool simply because it is popular.
